What makes a posting worth Gianluigi's time. `/find-jobs` uses this file to pick the best ≤20
of up to 50 postings for his Job Quest Board, to predict **fit** (the score he'd likely give,
1–10) and to fill each card's "Your preferences" checklist from the Scope and Learned sections.

## Scope (his words, 2026-11-07; change only when he says so)

From 1 Feb 2027 he works from home in Southern Italy as a business consultant for his parents'
companies, possibly alongside freelance [football mobile game] capture / project-management work. A job
must fit next to that.

- **Remote**, hiring someone who lives in **Italy** (Italy-only or EU-wide).
- **Part-time**, or **contract / freelance / fractional** clearly below full-time hours.
- One of three areas: **business consulting**, **data analytics**, **economics**.
- **Plan B, Puglia (his words, 2026-11-14):** full-time postings are in scope when the job
  is located in **Puglia** (on-site, hybrid or remote), still in one of the three areas.
  Remote part-time work stays the first choice.

## Hard excludes (straight to `seen`, with the reason; never on the board)

- Not remote (on-site, or hybrid with office days), or not open to someone based in Italy,
  unless the job is located in Puglia.
- Full-time employment only, unless the job is located in Puglia.
- Outside all three areas.
- Closed or no longer accepting applications.
- The current employer and its sister studios.
- A legal bar he can't meet (e.g. a role reserved for a protected category or nationality).

Everything else that might put him off is a **flag**, not an exclusion. He decides.

## Flags (shown under "Watch out" on the card)

German required (he has B1) · another language required · hours unclear · country restriction
unclear · seniority stretch (asks 5+ years) · pay not stated · agency or recruiter, end client unnamed.

## Starting guesses for Claude fit (Claude's priors, not his words)

Replace these as "Learned from his scores" grows; delete a guess once a learned line covers it.

- Core of an area (advisory work, analysis that drives a business decision, applied economics) → start at 6.
- Up: client-facing advice to small and mid-size firms; Italian market or Italian language; flexible hours;
  work that uses his evidence (econometrics, Python, commercial ownership of a product, reporting to CEOs).
- Down: data entry or AI-training annotation sold as "analytics"; sales or lead generation sold as
  "consulting"; commission-only pay; heavy German; seniority well beyond ~2 years.

RESEARCH

MSc Thesis — Evaluating the Effect of Germany's Nuclear Phase-Out on Renewable Energy Dispatch 2024

LMU Munich · Supervisor: [supervisor] · fully independent

  • Identification: April 2023 phase-out as a natural experiment.
  • Data: ~140,000 observations assembled from ENTSO-E, Network Transparency and futures market sources.
  • Methods: OLS, propensity score matching (implemented from scratch), K-means clustering, spatial grid congestion analysis across all four German TSO zones.
  • Central contribution: distinguished two rival mechanisms — reduced market value of renewables vs. increased grid congestion — using interacted regression models rather than asserting one.
  • Result: no aggregate effect on renewable dispatch, but a significant increase in onshore wind generation and higher day-ahead prices, implying a net welfare loss.
